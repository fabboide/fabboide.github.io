Say it without the word.

At this level fluency is not knowing more words. It is never getting stuck when the word you want does not arrive. Native speakers do not stop: they go around. That reflex is a skill, and it can be trained.

One Italian word, four forbidden words, sixty seconds. You explain it out loud and the microphone checks that you never touched the words you were not allowed to say.

you may not say

“È quel filo sottile che entra da una fessura quando pensavi che fosse tutto chiuso, e lo sentisci sul collo.”

how it works Press the microphone and start talking, in Italian. What you say appears on the screen as you speak. A forbidden word lights up red the moment it comes out. Keep going anyway: stopping is the only real mistake.

The page also counts your tools, the ready-made ways of going round a word: è quella cosa che…, serve per…, una specie di…, nel senso che… One point for each different tool you use. Ten rounds.

The microphone works in Chrome, Edge and Safari, not in Firefox. Your voice is transcribed by the browser's own speech service, so it leaves your computer the same way a web search does. Nothing is stored here: the transcript lives in this page and disappears when you close it. No microphone, or you would rather not: there is a stopwatch and your word of honour.
