The little words that do all the work.

Boh, mah, vabbè, anzi, appunto, magari, mica. They have no translation because they have no meaning: they have a job. Change the little word and the same sentence changes its mind.

This is the last thing to arrive and the first thing a native hears. You can be word-perfect and still sound like a textbook if these are missing.

the same four words, three different people

two directions Every card is a real exchange between two people. In Capisci you hear the little word and work out what the speaker is doing with it. In Dillo tu you get the attitude and have to supply the word.

Either way, the answer is never the point. What follows it is: the same line with the other three words in the same slot, and what each one would have turned it into. That contrast is the whole lesson.

A warning worth having: the voice button reads these lines with a synthetic voice, and half the meaning of boh, mah and beh lives in the intonation, which a synthetic voice flattens. Use it for the sentence around the word, and get the tune from a human.
