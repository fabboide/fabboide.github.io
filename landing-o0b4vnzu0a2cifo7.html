Questions

Before you ask.

Does it replace Preply?

No. It's a tool for you, not a marketplace. Keep teaching on Preply, italki or with your private students, and open the classroom during the lesson.

Do I really not need to prepare anything?

Every lesson comes with a 50-minute plan and all the activities, in order. Open it, glance at the plan, teach.

Which languages can I teach?

Spanish, French and Italian. Choose yours when you sign up.

Can the instructions be in my student's language?

Yes. Set the instructions in English, Español, Français, Italiano, Deutsch or Português, or go full immersion in the language you teach.

What do my students need?

A browser and the link to their page. No app, no account to set up, no password.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Move up when you get more students, move down when you need to.
